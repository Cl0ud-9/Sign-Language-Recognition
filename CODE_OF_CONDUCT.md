# Code of conduct

Sign Language Recognition is a small project. This page says how people are expected to treat each other in its issues,
pull requests and any other space that belongs to it.

## The short version

Be kind, be specific, and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

## What that looks like

- Describe problems and give feedback in a way the other person can act on. "The sign `my` is read as `hi` in dim
  light, here is a screenshot" helps; "it does not work" does not.
- Accept that people have different experience, first languages and time zones, and answer questions without making
  anyone feel small for asking.
- Respect the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community this project is about. Sign languages are full languages, not
  gestures.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment or discrimination of any kind, including mocking disability or how anyone
  signs.
- Sexualised language or attention, and anything meant to shock or intimidate.
- Publishing someone else's private information, including images or videos of people who did not agree to it.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, and pressure on the maintainer to act faster than they can.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
keep doing so, with or without warning depending on how serious it was.

To report behaviour that worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the address on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Cl0ud-9), or, if that is not possible, open a private report through the
[security form](https://github.com/Cl0ud-9/Sign-Language-Recognition/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct
report. Reports are handled quietly, and the person who reports is not named.

This applies in every space that belongs to the project, and when someone is clearly speaking for it elsewhere.
